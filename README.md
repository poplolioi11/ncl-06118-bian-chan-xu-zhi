# 便產須知（臺灣國家圖書館 06118） / Bian chan xu zhi [Essentials for Childbirth]: Transcription Dataset

《便產須知》是臺灣國家圖書館藏清代婦產科抄本，藏號 06118，卷首有道光甲午（1834）識語。上下卷涉及調經、妊娠、難產、產後照護與歷史病例。下卷之後另抄《廣嗣方附》與《時痘疹精粹》等，可據以追查同冊配抄的醫療知識與編排。

Bian chan xu zhi is a Qing obstetric manuscript held by the National Central Library, Taiwan (06118), with a prefatory inscription dated 1834. Its two volumes cover menstruation, pregnancy, difficult labour, postpartum care and historical cases. The same physical copy also contains separately titled texts on reproduction and childhood illness.

依同一份 58 頁影像 PDF 保存封面、識語、上下卷、後附文字、印記及空白面順序。

The complete 58-page image PDF is indexed, including the cover, inscription, two volumes, appended texts, ownership marks and blank surfaces.

## 文字與覆核 / Text and review

58幅逐幅回圖盤點，另讀題記、條首、夾注與疑字，依字形採用 11 項訂正。覆核為逐幅選讀。未做人類全文雙校、專家審定、同儕審查或準確率量測。PDF 頁碼固定，展開面依右、左葉分列。留疑的讀字可依幅、面與欄定位。圖像水印另作現代掃描記號。

All 58 images received an image review and a selective rereading of inscriptions, headings, marginal writing and difficult characters. 11 image-supported changes are documented. Human full-text double proofreading, expert review, peer review and accuracy measurement were not performed. PDF page numbers are fixed. Facing leaves are distinguished by side. Uncertain readings refer to their leaf and column, while digitization watermarks are recorded separately.

TXT 依幅、面與欄提供閱讀定位。JSON 和 TEI 採同一固定取文。「□」標難辨字，缺幅或不確定缺字處由頁面說明及疑義資料標示。完整提示、私有覆核過程與專用程式未隨附。AI 實際模型路由與用量未知。
